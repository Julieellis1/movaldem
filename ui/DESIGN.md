## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an immersive, transcendent digital sanctuary crafted for contemporary ministry. Built upon deep obsidian canvas architecture, subtle ambient violet glows, and warm sacred gold accents, the experience evokes reverence, warmth, belonging, and forward-looking spiritual growth.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Dark Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Minimalist UI**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Multi-tiered charcoal and obsidian surfaces paired with ultra-diffused atmospheric ambient backdrops.
- **Luminous Hierarchy:** Spiritual warmth projected through luminous violet (#8B5CF6) highlights, secondary radiant orchid tones, and divine amber/gold focal points (#F59E0B) for stewardship and giving.
- **Modern Approachability:** High-clarity typography paired with pill-shaped action surfaces that feel tactile, welcoming, and intuitive across devices.

## Colors

The palette establishes a sacred, dark-mode-first environment where typography and glowing elements emerge seamlessly from velvety obsidian surfaces.

- **Primary (`#8B5CF6`):** The primary violet evokes spiritual transcendence, contemplation, and modern vibrancy. It powers key brand touchpoints, active states, active tab highlights, and luminous button borders.
- **Secondary (`#A855F7`):** Radiant orchid supports linear gradient flows across hero sections, featured badge highlights, and interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm gold / amber introduces holy radiance, reserved specifically for calls-to-action regarding stewardship, Online Giving (Paystack flow), live stream indicators, and Bible quiz achievements.
- **Surfaces (`#0D0D11` to `#242735`):** Charcoal-to-obsidian steps provide clear dimensional separation without requiring stark outlines.
- **Text & Accents:** Pure white text is preserved strictly for primary headings and prominent pill CTAs, while subdued warm grays ensure comfort during extended devotional reading and scripture study.

## Typography

The typography couples the welcoming geometric personality of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision and optical balance of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Conveys warm authority and approachability. Generous x-height and gentle organic curves prevent spiritual text from feeling overly severe. Tight negative tracking on display sizes enhances structural coherence against dark backdrops.
- **Body & Scripture (Inter):** High clarity at standard reading densities ensures optimal legibility during scripture references, sermon transcripts, and extended devotional reflections.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Rendered in uppercase or semi-bold pill badges to drive crisp, organized content taxonomies across ministry ministry categories (e.g., "SERMON", "SUNDAY SCHOOL", "PRAYER REQUEST").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a controlled 12-column grid system optimized for immersive content flow, media delivery, and community interactions.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column desktop grid with a max-width container of `1280px` (`80rem`), centering content with generous atmospheric margins. On mobile devices, transitions into a flexible 4-column flow with reduced gutters (`1rem`) to maximize media display area.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous spacing (`space-xl` through `margin`) creates distinct sanctuaries of content, preventing cognitive overload during worship media consumption.
- **Section Grouping:** Functional modules (Sermon highlights, Event registration, Sunday School, and Giving) utilize card-based internal padding (`space-md` on mobile, `space-lg` to `space-xl` on desktop) to retain airy visual room around scripture and prayer requests.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; instead, it is constructed through luminous tonal layering, frosted dark glass surfaces, and subtle colored rim lights.

- **Ground Level (`#0D0D11`):** The primary canvas upon which all experiences rest.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#13141B`):** Base cards and interactive containers with an ultra-thin boundary outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1B1D27`):** Elevated modals, dropdown drawers, and active hover cards, featuring an inner radial glow tinted with primary violet (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Level 3 / Glassmorphic Overlays:** Sticky navigation bars and floating media control bars employ `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on `rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.75)` with a hairline top border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Sacred Glow Effects:** Featured sermons and active Giving CTA tiers employ soft multi-stage outer glows: `0 0 32px -8px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an open, friendly **Pill-Shaped (Level 3)** geometry that invites physical interaction and touch.

- **Buttons & Tags:** Strictly pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), delivering a human, non-institutionalized feel for action buttons ("Watch", "Give", "Join Group", "Read More").
- **Cards & Media Tile Containers:** Styled with `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px) or `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px), creating distinct capsule-like frames around video sermons, event photos, and quiz categories.
- **Inputs & Field Enclosures:** Follow the soft capsule standard (`border-radius: 9999px` for single-line inputs and pills; `rounded-lg` for multiline prayer submission textareas).

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Pill:** Background in rich violet (`#8B5CF6`) or full white with pure black typography for supreme focal contrast. Fully rounded ends (`9999px`), `12px 28px` padding.
- **Outlined Sacred Pill:** Transparent surface enclosed in a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or `#8B5CF6`) that transitions to a full violet tint and soft glow on hover.
- **Sacred Giving CTA (Paystack Action):** Accentuated in warm gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B, #D97706)`) with dark charcoal text for distinct financial/tithe actioning.

### Cards & Sermon Tiles
- **Media Cards:** Charcoal surface (`#13141B`) with 32px rounded corners, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), overflow hidden, and high-impact full-bleed photography. Hover states introduce a soft violet rim lighting (`border-color: rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`).
- **Featured Sermon Banner:** Immersive dark backdrop with integrated play button overlay, pill metadata badge ("LATEST SERMON"), bold Plus Jakarta Sans title, and direct devotion link.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips:** Ultra-compact pill components (`label-sm`), featuring semi-translucent fills (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`) with crisp violet text (`#C79CFF`) and uppercase letter spacing. Used across Bible quiz categories, sermon tags, and event dates.

### Input Fields & Giving Controls
- **Donation Preset Pills:** Horizontal cluster of monetary amount pills ($20, $50, $100, Custom) with immediate radio-like selection feedback (solid violet fill with gold perimeter on active selection).
- **Text Inputs:** Dark capsule fields (`#1B1D27`) with inset padding (`16px 24px`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, focusing smoothly to `#8B5CF6` with an ambient glow ring.

### Bible Quiz & Sunday School Modules
- **Interactive Option Rows:** Stacked full-width rounded pill containers providing distinct resting, hovered, selected, and correct/incorrect state glows (using emerald for right and crimson for wrong).
- **Progress Trackers:** Slender pill progress lines with a vibrant purple-to-gold linear gradient fill.
